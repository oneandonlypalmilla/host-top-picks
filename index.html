Weekly programme, experience menu or one-pagers. The app reads each activity, the dates or days it runs, times, duration and price. Anything unclear about dates is flagged for you to confirm before saving.

Publish

Publish the programme to all Hosts

  1. Upload and check the activities in the Activities tab.
  2. Click Download activities.json below.
  3. In the GitHub repository, click Add file → Upload files, drop activities.json in and click Commit changes (it replaces the previous one).

Hosts receive the new programme automatically the next time they open or return to the app — usually within a few minutes of the upload.
